# Weekly Theme Notes

Weekly notes are the manual bridge between noisy daily imports and durable knowledge.
They are intentionally fewer, denser, and more opinionated than the imported day files.

## Promotion rules

Promote something into a weekly note only if it survives more than one headline cycle.
Good candidates usually connect to one or more topic MOCs such as:

- [[Topics/Rates and Fed|Rates and Fed]]
- [[Topics/Tech|Tech]]
- [[Topics/Energy|Energy]]
- [[Topics/War and Geopolitics|War and Geopolitics]]
- [[Topics/Financials|Financials]]
- [[Topics/Retail and Consumer|Retail and Consumer]]

## What stays out

- one-off headline clutter
- broken scraper/API runs
- obvious duplicate catalyst blurbs
